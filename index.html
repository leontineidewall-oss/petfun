How it works

A coin you have to keep alive.

01 · hatch

Launch, and an egg appears

Coins launch on pump.fun through pet.fun. pet.fun is set as the creator, so 100% of the creator fees belong to the pet, not a dev.

02 · feed

Fees are its food

Every 10 minutes the fees are claimed and spent buying the coin back. The bought coins are burned. That is a meal, and every meal is posted with its transaction.

03 · grow or starve

Grow up, or become a gravestone

1 SOL eaten lays the egg, 10 hatches a baby, 100 makes it grown. 24 hours without a meal and it dies. Holders lose nothing but the pet.

FAQ

Questions.

Is pet.fun live?

Not yet. The box at the top is a preview: paste any coin already on pump.fun and it shows the pet that coin would have, using its real trading data from DexScreener. The nursery shows pets for pump.fun coins trending on DexScreener right now.

How is "fed in last 24h" worked out?

24h volume in USD, divided by the SOL price, times the creator fee pump.fun charges at the coin's current market cap (0.30% on the bonding curve, 0.95% down to 0.05% on PumpSwap). The real fee depends on the market cap at each trade, so it is an estimate.

Why does the preview use 24 hours?

Public data only gives the last 24 hours of volume. A real pet.fun pet counts every meal since launch, so it only ever grows. Its hunger is the part that resets.

Does a dead pet hurt holders?

No. Nothing is taken from wallets. The pet turns into a gravestone on the chart and stays one. Coins already burned stay burned.

Does every coin look different?

Yes. The pet's colour, cheeks and sprout come from the coin's address, so each coin always hatches the same pet.

What happens when a coin graduates?

Meals keep going on PumpSwap. The creator fee there starts at 0.95% and falls as the market cap rises, so big coins eat a smaller share of a bigger volume.
